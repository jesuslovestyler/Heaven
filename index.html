Heaven

What awaits those who love God — told by the Bible itself, in its own words.

Nothing added, nothing guessed. Only what Scripture says.

A place prepared for you

“In my Father’s house are many homes. If it weren’t so, I would have told you. I am going to prepare a place for you. If I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and will receive you to myself; that where I am, you may be there also.”

John 14:2–3

Heaven is not a prize held out for the strong. It is a home being made ready by Jesus Himself — and His promise is not only a place prepared, but a Person coming back: where I am, you may be there also.

✦

No more tears

“Behold, God’s dwelling is with people, and he will dwell with them, and they will be his people, and God himself will be with them as their God. He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. Death will be no more; neither will there be mourning, nor crying, nor pain, any more. The first things have passed away.”

“Behold, I am making all things new.”

Revelation 21:3–5

God does not explain the tears away. He wipes them — every one of them — and death, mourning, crying, and pain go with the first things that have passed away. The One who made all things is making all things new.

✦

No more night

“There will be no curse any more. The throne of God and of the Lamb will be in it, and his servants will serve him. They will see his face, and his name will be on their foreheads. There will be no night, and they need no lamp light or light of the sun; for the Lord God will illuminate them. They will reign forever and ever.”

Revelation 22:3–5

No curse, no darkness, no lamp needed. God Himself is the light there — and His servants will see His face, the sight every human heart was made for.

✦

The Lamb in the middle

“They will never be hungry or thirsty any more. The sun won’t beat on them, nor any heat; for the Lamb who is in the middle of the throne shepherds them and leads them to springs of life-giving waters. And God will wipe away every tear from their eyes.”

Revelation 7:16–17

At the very center of the throne is a Lamb — and the Lamb is a Shepherd. He leads His people Himself to springs of life-giving waters, and no hunger, no thirst, and no scorching heat will ever touch them again.

✦

Beyond imagining

“But as it is written, ‘Things which an eye didn’t see, and an ear didn’t hear, which didn’t enter into the heart of man, these God has prepared for those who love him.’”

1 Corinthians 2:9

Whatever we picture, Scripture says it falls short. Eye has not seen it and ear has not heard it — what God has prepared for those who love Him is beyond the reach of our best imagining.

✦

God himself will be with them

Heaven is not first a place. It is a Person’s home — the Father’s house — and the best of it is not anything in it, but Someone: God himself will be with them as their God. No one earns a home there. Citizenship in heaven is given, by faith in Jesus, to everyone who comes to Him.

“For our citizenship is in heaven, from where we also wait for a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ.”

Philippians 3:20
